## Phase Goal

**As a** site owner, **I want to** update GigPress without losing or corrupting existing records, relationships, settings, or show-management behavior, **so that** my established site remains usable after the update.

<objective>
Preserve the existing show-management lifecycle after upgrade and repair the two relationship hazards identified by research: dependency deletion that ignores trash, and tour undo that cannot identify ownership.

Purpose: Complete DATA-02 through the existing procedural handlers and data model while enforcing Plan 02-01's incomplete-migration boundary at the mutation layer.
Output: Safe create/edit/copy/trash/restore behavior, active-and-trashed artist/venue deletion guards, ownership-aware tour undo, and one containerized mutation probe module.
</objective>
